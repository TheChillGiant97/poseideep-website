Why Private?

More than having a
dive guide to yourself.

Private diving changes more than the size of the group. It gives us the freedom to shape the experience around the people actually doing the dive.

01

Your Pace

Take the time you need. No pressure to keep up with an unfamiliar group or follow someone else's rhythm.

02

Your Interests

Spend more of your dive doing what interests you — whether that's exploring, observing marine life, photography or simply enjoying the experience.

03

Your Objectives

From relaxed recreational diving and confidence building to skill development and more advanced objectives, the dive can be shaped around what you want from it.

04

Your Experience

Dive planning takes your certification, experience and comfort level into account, rather than treating every diver the same.

Your Dive

Shaped around
what you want from it.

Private diving gives us the flexibility to create an experience around you — whether you're looking for relaxed exploration, something more personal, or a more advanced dive.

01

Private Guided Diving

One-to-one diving or diving with your own private group, with the pace and experience planned around the divers rather than a larger group.

02

Personalised Experiences

Reef exploration, wrecks, photography, returning after time away from diving, or simply taking your time underwater. Tell us what interests you and we can shape the experience accordingly.

03

Advanced & Technical Diving

Private diving can extend beyond recreational exploration. For suitably qualified divers, more advanced objectives can be considered according to experience, conditions and dive planning.
